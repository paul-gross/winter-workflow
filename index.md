# Winter workflow

- **ADM review for agent-facing configuration** — after creating or modifying agent-facing *configuration* (agent definitions, skills, commands, `CLAUDE.md`, `ai/` conventions and docs), spawn the `agentic-development-manager` agent to review before committing. This does **not** apply to product backlog plans, approaches, or product-centered initiatives describing future vision or roadmaps — those are reviewed by the user and downstream implementation agents.
